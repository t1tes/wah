Letter 1: I love you

Happy 18th Birthday, Jaymiso. Honestly, why not 18 letters? Writing one every week accidentally became a routine for me, but more than that, I wanted to create something you could come back to. I know I won't be there physically to celebrate your birthday with you, and that honestly sucks because I wish I could be there for all the small moments too, not just the big ones. So instead, I made you this website, something you can revisit whenever you feel down, whenever things get overwhelming, or whenever I can't be there beside you to remind you that you're not alone. I still remember you mentioning how badly you wanted to make a website one day, so I thought, why not make one for you first? It took a lot of learning, a lot of frustration, and probably a few broken keycaps along the way, but somehow you're here reading this now. I guess that makes it worth it. It feels strange that I get to be part of the year you turn 18, Jayme. Like, out of all the years of your life, I happened to meet you right before this one. I know you've said before that turning 18 probably won't feel as big as people make it seem. On the outside, it's just another day. You'll be in the Philippines, surrounded by people and places that are familiar but also somehow unfamiliar after everything you've experienced. Maybe it won't feel like some magical transformation where everything suddenly changes. Maybe you'll wake up and still feel like yourself. Maybe you'll still have the same thoughts, the same worries, the same questions. But I hope you realize that even if it feels like just another day, it isn't. Because I got to witness a part of your life that led you here. I've watched you question things, try things, change your mind, discover new parts of yourself, and still keep going even when you weren't sure where you were headed. I think people would call that maturity, but I don't think that word fully explains it. To me, it's something more personal than that. It's the way you refuse to let your fears completely decide your life. You have this habit of wanting to experience everything. You want to try things right there and then. You get curious about something and suddenly you're already halfway into it. Sometimes I think you don't even realize how much of yourself you put into the things you care about. And I think that's one of the things I admire most about you. Not because you're perfect. You're definitely not, and I know you'd probably laugh if I said that. You're weird, you're stubborn, you overthink, you have your moments where you make absolutely no sense, but somehow those are the same things that make you Jayme. I love the way your mind works. The way you can look at something ordinary and somehow find something else in it. The way you can make a random conversation turn into something I'll still think about hours later. The way you chase things because they interest you, even when you're scared of where they might lead. Sometimes I think about the little kid version of you. The one who had no idea what life was going to become, who didn't know the things she would experience, the people she would meet, or the person she would eventually grow into. And I just know that little kid would be proud of you. Not because everything went perfectly. It didn't. Not because you never struggled. You did. But because somehow, after everything, you're still here. Still trying. Still learning. Still loving things deeply. And now you're 18. Which is honestly crazy to think about because I remember meeting you and never expecting that you would become someone I'd care about this much. I didn't know that someone could become such a big part of my everyday life. I didn't know that I'd remember the little things, the random conversations, the jokes, the moments that probably didn't mean anything to anyone else but somehow stayed with me. So happy birthday, Jayme. I hope when you read these letters, you remember that they exist because I wanted you to have something that stays. Something you can look back on and know that during this part of your life, someone was here cheering for you. Even from far away. Especially from far away. I hope you have a good birthday, Jaymiso. I hope you let yourself enjoy it. I hope you eat something good, laugh until your stomach hurts, and make memories you'll want to keep. And if you ever forget how far you've come, come back here. You'll always have these 18 letters waiting for you. I love you. Happy 18th birthday.

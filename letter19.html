Letter 19

Jayme, I soup you is basically what the last 18 letters have been saying, if you didn't just skip straight to this one. We were supposed to spend your birthday together. Instead, I stayed up the whole day, knowing I couldn't greet you, contemplating if you'd even want this. So far, August now feels like the true ending of a year. Everything in August feels like you, too warm, too late, too close to ending. This letter wasn't originally supposed to exist, but you shouldn't have to wait for closure, and I know you hoped you'd find the truth somewhere. Bear with me if this feels too much like a journal. I'm doing this for you, but I'm doing this for me, too, a last attempt to get what little truth is left out of me before I let you go. I should have told you back then. Even if you would've left, I was already losing you in the moments I chose not to tell you. I know I would've never told you. I'd rather have us pretending now than pretend the silence is doing us a favor. I guess that's what happens when we were always bleeding into each other so much that we didn't know where you began and I ended. I've always had this longing for you, and I didn't know how to handle it. Then he came along, our friends put us together, and the feeling came quicker to him than it did to me. For some reason, I just didn't know how to say no. Who could blame me for seeing only what I wanted to see? I loved everything that didn't love me back; it was the easiest thing in the world. Back then, I believed in change, I believed scaffolding was the same thing as structure, and I thought I could build it. Then you suddenly appeared and it felt like that scaffolding was so easy to abandon, so natural I could just leave. I didn't have to fake anything, I didn't have to be so reserved, and you knew just what to say. You know how much I cherish my time; I wouldn't waste it on you if I didn't want to spend my life with you. After all, you were my birthday wish. I looked at him and thought: if I was very brave or very honest, I would say it so he would know it and I would know it and I could never take it back. But I wasn't that brave or honest, so all I did was look at him, and I think you knew anyway. I didn't know how to say no. Maybe it's because I knew you were leaving and I used it as a failsafe for when something bad inevitably happened. Something did. I was awaiting a tragedy that already occurred and I only chose the outcome for myself and not for us, and I regret that terribly. Maybe the reason I kept a failsafe was because I didn't actually think we'd last when you went away. I overthought about it all until I was actually living it, and the guilt never stopped feeling suffocating. Everything I said I liked about you is everything I didn't choose in him. I've been trying to make it right. I tried to end it with him shortly after, but it didn't work as I expected since it was so public. I just might do it again along the way. I wanted to explain myself in an understandable way, so I gave shape to my fears and made excuses, living in the worst parts, ruining myself. I was afraid that no one was going to accept me, something I've been fighting since the beginning, fighting something that already won a long time ago. I was afraid they would figure me out, that they would discover who I was and say it. Above all, there was a great shame, not only of being me, but simply of being. I think there's a point in which it becomes too late to help some people, a time past which the damage has calcified so completely that there's no undoing it. You could say there's still time to figure it out, but to be honest, I don't think I'll even try anymore when I've lost the first person that made me defend myself against my parents. Yes, I told them. And no, my friends knew everything I was doing to you the whole time, they knew I'd be happier with someone like you. I knew that I was doing it, but I'm incapable of doing what needs to be done. I turned a blind eye to all of it and chose something safer, something that would be normal to everyone. Sometimes I sit down and feel jealous of you, the way you can just easily walk around and not care about what people think because you've already walked out the door, while my identity is still asking for permission. And there's a big part of me that wishes I never met you, I preferred the emptiness in me when I didn't know it was there. Every day I've stayed up since, thinking that maybe tomorrow I'd wake up sure of what I wasn't with you, but tomorrow just became another yesterday. I sleep so late and wake up so late because I have no one to dream about, nor do I have anyone to be awake for. Lying to myself doesn't work anymore because deep down I know who it is I want, I know they exist, and I know they exist without me. At this point, it's not about finding someone to replace you; I have spread my love all over the place. It's about trying to sleep knowing I live in a world that has your hands in it. Maybe one day me and my friends are sitting around somewhere and they ask me who I love, and my parents used to ask me who I loved, and I'll think of you, I'll be thinking of you, I've always thought of you, and I'll tell them, "No one." Maybe I'll always live in the wish that we'd have one more conversation, one more chance, one version of tomorrow that I haven't already imagined a million times so it feels realer than today. Maybe it was my fault for thinking we were going to be something more. This isn't me justifying it; it's my problem, not my why. You probably regret it and wish you listened to everyone else. I wish you did, too. Yet I wouldn't trade the smallest amount of what we had for anything else. I took advantage of that, and now I have no one to tell that I'm afraid. Maybe "I love you" is just another word to me, that I use it so lightheartedly. Maybe you're right. I wanted to call you that night again; I thought the silence would somehow answer your questions, but I knew that if you answered, I'd have told you I loved you, and I don't think you would've said it back. I don't know if the things you've been doing have been to spite me or to check if you're still stuck there. If this letter in any way gives you the thought of reaching out: go out, get drunk, get high, kiss whoever you want. I'm helpless anyway. You probably thought and wondered: What was I doing all this time? And why do I say nothing? That I'm evil, I know, and sometimes when I smiled at you, you hated me. You wanted to strike me. Wanted to make me bleed. I smiled at you the way I smiled at everyone, I told you what I told everyone, and I tell you nothing but lies, that you have never reached me, I have never really been there. But I do not think I have ever lied to you, though I know that I have never told you the truth. Why? What was I always hiding? And you thought that I did not know when I made love to you, I was making love to no one? No one. Or everyone, but not you, certainly. You were nothing to me, nothing, and I bring you fever but no delight. You were the only person I've ever even hesitated to hug, and you were the only girl that has seen me with my shirt off. You're moving on and I'm being really brave about it. You have all the reasons to leave, knowing that leaving was never an option to you before. I guess it was always a different kind of trust, one I couldn't find in myself to instill in you. Everyone is a monster to someone, and since you are so convinced that I am yours, I will be it. I'll keep continuing to live in the past because the present does not fulfill me. The void in my heart can only be filled with old voices that are now just echoes, the chilled blood in my body can only be warmed by old hands, and the weeping soul at my feet can only be healed by promises that are now broken. I linger for the feeling of looking into your eyes again. I linger for not a lot, but only you. Your mouth left marks on my neck, and I wish they were still visible, proof that you loved me once. It's like I'm permanently outside myself. I hope one day, if ever we're in the same place at the same time, and I smell a familiar smell in the crowd, or see the way your hair moves while you walk, or the nape of your neck, I hope I shift my focus to the person standing next to you that'll never be me. But if I even gain the courage to be even a meter behind you and called out your name, would you recognize my voice? Would you still look back at me? I have so much I want to ask about you, but I want you to be happy and do everything we promised each other we'd do, maybe even more than that. And I hope I never hear anything about it. I'm sorry I put you through this. I'm sorry if a part of me still wishes we could start from a place not where it all fell apart, but one where we both believed that this could work out, and that you'll hold my hand until I'm myself. grab my wrists with both your fists and tell me your favorite lies of mine. Maybe in five years our paths will cross again, and I will tell you how desperately in love I was with you, and we can laugh about how I broke your heart. But I am giving you space. You will come through when it is right for you; it's the least that I owe you after all of this. In the meantime, I'm looking for you at the edge of the garden. I'm looking for you every time I come home. I'm looking for you at the end of my story. I'm looking for you. I think that you know. I might be a minute for you, but you were everything to me. I loved you, just not enough for me to love myself. I love you more than my own skin, and even though you don't love me the same way, you loved me anyway, didn't you? And if you don't, I'll always have the hope that you do, and I'm okay with that. Love me a little. Happy birthday. It's over for the sake of letting you go. Love is an old-fashioned word anyway. I adore you. Do you hate me now? You're probably wondering what the fuck is wrong with me. The cruelest thing I could do is ruin the first day of you being 18. But I don't really have anything left to lose anymore, and I'd regret staying silent just as much as I'd regret saying something. Besides... I already bought the Yamaha guitar you wanted.
